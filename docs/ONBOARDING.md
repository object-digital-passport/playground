# Partner onboarding

1. Select a scenario or propose a new one; state the business problem, participants and success criteria.
2. Explain what ODP verifies (record provenance and document integrity) and what it does not verify (physical authenticity, ownership, real-world truth or legal compliance).
3. Walk through the wallet-free demo with synthetic records; demo is planned, not available yet.
4. Use dedicated test wallets on Amoy or Localnet; provision test tokens without exposing keys. Record onboarding friction.
5. If a real-money pilot is justified, follow the approval and limits in `MAINNET_PILOT.md` before any Mainnet write.
6. Export permitted `.odpass` fixtures and collect anonymized feedback; return protocol gaps to Specifications and findings to Research.

The repository is public; partner authentication and authorization belong to the application. A future separately hosted pilot environment can be invitation-only. Never publish real partner data, credentials or restricted code.
